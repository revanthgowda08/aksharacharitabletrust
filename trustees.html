Board Structure

How the Board of Trustees works

👥

Board Size

Managed by a Board of Trustees with a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 9 trustees.

➕

Appointments

The Managing Trustee can appoint up to 6 additional trustees, each serving a renewable 3-year tenure.

🗳️

Meetings & Quorum

The Board meets at least once every 3 months. Quorum is 2 trustees or 60% of members, whichever is greater; decisions pass by simple majority.

📩

Notice & Circulation

Meetings require one week's advance notice. Resolutions can also be passed by circulation, with two-thirds of trustees consenting in writing.

🖋️

Correspondence

All official correspondence is signed by the Managing Trustee or an authorised signatory acting on the Board's behalf.

🏦

Banking Authority

The Managing Trustee operates local accounts individually; foreign-contribution accounts need a joint signature with the second founder trustee.

Accountability

When a trustee ceases to hold office

The Trust Deed sets out clear conditions under which a trustee's position ends, and how the resulting vacancy is filled.

Death or Resignation

A trustee's office ends on death or voluntary resignation.

Insolvency

Office ends if a trustee is declared insolvent by a court.

Conviction

Office ends on conviction for an offence involving moral turpitude.

Acting Against the Trust

Office ends if a trustee acts in a manner prejudicial to the Trust's interests.

Vacancies arising from any of the above are filled by nomination from the Trust's remaining trustee members.
