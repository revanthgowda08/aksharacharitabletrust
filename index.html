Who We Are

A trust founded to serve, not to profit.

Akshara Charitable Trust was established on 6th March 2020 in Bangalore as a Public Charitable Trust under the Indian Trust Act, 1882, with an initial corpus contributed by its founder trustee. It is registered with the Income Tax Department (PAN: AAHTA9161F) and files its returns as an Association of Persons every year.

No trustee, member or committee official may personally benefit from the Trust's funds or income. Every rupee raised is directed back into the Trust's charitable objects — education, vocational training, health, rural development, environment and social welfare — for the benefit of the public at large.

What We Work On

Objects of the Trust, at a glance

Clause 6 of the Trust Deed sets out a wide charter — from schools and colleges to rural livelihoods and disaster relief. Here are the core areas we build programmes around.

🎓

Education & Vocational Training

Nursery to postgraduate institutions, polytechnics, ITIs and skill-development centres in tailoring, computing, food processing and more.

🩺

Health & Alternative Medicine

Free medical camps, Ayurveda and homeopathy promotion, de-addiction support and HIV/AIDS awareness programmes.

🌾

Rural Development

Watershed development, rainwater harvesting, organic farming, dairy and horticulture support, and women's self-help groups.

🏠

Institutional Care

Hostels, day-care and short-stay homes, old-age homes and orphanages for those who need shelter and support.

🌳

Environment

Social forestry, afforestation drives, tree planting in schools, waste management and environmental education.

⚖️

Legal Aid & Human Rights

Legal awareness camps, child-labour prevention, women's safety initiatives and road-safety street plays.
