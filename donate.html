Where It Goes

What your donation could fund

Akshara Charitable Trust's objects span education, health, rural development and social welfare. A donation of any size helps move real, on-record efforts — like the Trust's request for land to build a Pharmacy, Nursing and Paramedical institution — closer to reality.

₹500School supplies or a free medical-camp kit for one family
₹2,500A day of a skill-training or literacy session for a small group
₹5,000+Materials toward a rural-development or afforestation initiative

On tax deductibility. Akshara Charitable Trust's 80G exemption registration is in progress (see Governing Clauses). Donation receipts are issued for every contribution; 80G tax-deduction certificates will be available once that registration is complete.

Bank transfers & CSR/institutional giving. For NEFT/RTGS, cheque donations, or corporate CSR partnerships, please contact the Trust directly — our Managing Trustee will share verified account details and documentation.
