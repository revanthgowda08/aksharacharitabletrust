Governing Clauses

Nature of the Trust & how it operates

🤝

Nature of the Trust

A Public Trust for educational, social, cultural, charitable and economic welfare purposes.

⚖️

Charitable Clause

Benefits are open to all sections of society, with no bar on caste, colour or sex, per the Income Tax Act, 1961.

🚫

No Private Benefit

Trust funds or income cannot personally benefit any trustee; investments may only generate income for the Trust's welfare programmes.

📜

Amendment Clause

No amendment may conflict with Sections 2(15), 11, 12, 12A, 13, 80G or 30 of the Income Tax Act, 1961; amendments require prior Income Tax Commissioner approval.

🛡️

Indemnity

Trustees are indemnified for actions taken in good faith and within their authority.

🔄

Dissolution

On winding up, remaining assets transfer to another 80G/35-recognised charitable trust — never distributed to trustees or committee members.

FCRA & tax registration. The Trust intends to register with the Ministry of Home Affairs under FCRA 1976 and with the Income Tax Department for exemption under Sections 10(22)A, 10(23)A, 10(22)B, 80, 11, 12, 12A and 35. Books of accounts are maintained and audited annually by a Chartered Accountant after the close of each financial year.
Funds & Financial Management

How the Trust is funded and run

Sources of Funds

Member subscriptions, cultural-programme proceeds, loans or deposits from banks, companies, individuals, firms and public trusts, donations, gifts, government grants (state and central), and foreign contributions.

Foreign Contributions

Handled under FCRA 1976 through a separate dedicated bank account, with NIL returns filed when applicable.

Investment & Borrowing

Funds are invested per Sections 13(1)(d) and 11(5) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The Managing Trustee is authorised to borrow via loans, deposits or debentures on suitable terms.

Signing Authority

The Managing Trustee operates local bank accounts individually; foreign-transaction accounts require a joint signature with the second founder trustee.

Board Meetings

The Board meets at least once every three months. Quorum is 2 trustees or 60% of total members, whichever is greater, with decisions by simple majority and one week's advance notice.

Correspondence

All official correspondence is carried out under the signature of the Managing Trustee or an authorised signatory.
